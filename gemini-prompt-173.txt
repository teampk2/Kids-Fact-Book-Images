Issue: Entire Image Turns Black When "Remove Inner White Parts" Is Enabled
Location:
"Single Preset Renderer" section — specifically the "Remove Inner White Parts" option.

Current Behavior (Bug):
Regarding the code implemented earlier, when the user enables the "Remove Inner White Parts" option and uploads an SVG file, the internal white parts do not simply disappear as intended. Instead, the entire image turns black, and the layer shapes also become black — meaning the removal logic is incorrectly affecting the whole artwork rather than only the targeted white elements.

Expected Behavior:
When the option is enabled, only the specific internal white parts and white elements (or any other specifically targeted colors) should be removed — meaning they should become transparent or disappear. The rest of the image, including all other colors, layers, shapes, and design elements, must remain completely untouched and preserved. The entire image must not turn black, and no other elements should be affected.

Fix Required:

Ensure the removal logic targets only the specified internal white parts and elements (or the specific targeted color).

Removed elements must become transparent or eliminated — not converted to black.

The entire image must never turn black or lose its original colors.

All non-targeted elements, layers, shapes, and colors must remain fully intact and unchanged.

Ensure the fix works correctly both independently and in combination with the existing "Remove Background" option.
