### Full Book Manuscript – Clear All Existing Books and Start Fresh

In the **Full Book Manuscript** section, completely remove **all books that have already been generated and saved**, regardless of how many books currently exist.

### Required Implementation

* Delete **all existing Full Book Manuscript records** from the application.
* Remove all previously generated book entries from the manuscript/library list.
* Remove the associated old metadata, saved records, and references.
* The old books must **not reappear** after refreshing the page, restarting the app, or generating a new book.
* Do not retain any old book numbering, sequence, or cached manuscript records.
* Do not delete or modify the underlying book-generation functionality itself.

### Start Fresh

After the cleanup:

* Start the **Full Book Manuscript** library from a completely clean state.
* The next newly generated book should be treated as the **first new book** in the fresh sequence.
* Apply the new filename and synchronization rules to all books generated from this point forward.
* Ensure the cleared old records cannot be restored automatically from stale local storage, cache, database records, or previous sync metadata.

### Expected Result

The **Full Book Manuscript** section should contain **zero old generated books** after the cleanup. From that point onward, every newly generated book should be added as a **new, fresh manuscript**, with no old books appearing again.
